# Frozen Narrative Themes Specification

Narrative themes describe documented cultural and symbolic themes associated with carpet motifs and weaving traditions.

## Theme Definitions

| Code | Canonical Key | Icon & Turkish UI Label | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| **N1** | `nature_growth` | 🌿 Doğa & Büyüme | Flora, botanical motifs, life cycle, and natural vitality |
| **N2** | `belonging_home` | 🏠 Aidiyet & Yuva | Protection, sanctuary, tribal identity, and home hearth |
| **N3** | `journey_discovery` | 🧭 Yolculuk & Keşif | Migration routes, nomadic paths, horizons, and spiritual quests |
| **N4** | `continuity_connection` | 🔗 Süreklilik & Bağ | Interlocking patterns, lineage, family ties, and eternal loops |
| **N5** | `balance_order` | ⚖️ Denge & Düzen | Harmonic symmetry, cosmic order, dualities, and proportion |
| **N6** | `renewal_transformation` | ✨ Yenilenme & Dönüşüm | Seasonal shifts, rebirth, water flow, and protective talismans |
| **N7** | `tradition_craft` | 🧵 Gelenek & Zanaat | Dye techniques, weaving mastery, loom heritage, and skill |
| **N8** | `cultural_heritage` | 🌍 Kültürel Miras | Regional archetypes, historic iconography, and documented provenance |

## Rules & Integrity

1. **Fixed Taxonomy**: The 8 narrative themes represent the canonical thematic classifier space.
2. **Sourced Motifs Only**: Motifs associated with these themes must be backed by published ethnographic/textile literature.
3. **No Mythmaking**: Fictional, psychological, or invented motif interpretations are strictly prohibited.
